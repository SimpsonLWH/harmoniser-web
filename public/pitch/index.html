Capsules that stay live.

Live weather

Weather for 12 cities, cached for 15 minutes, with clear offline and out-of-date states.

Real reminders

Timers and reminders become system calendar events, so they fire even with the app closed.

Phone sensors

Shake to count, and read the battery level. Only while the app is open, and nothing is recorded.

Widgets that work

Pin any capsule to the home screen. Its buttons work right there.

From your phone to anywhere.

QR codes and files

Share a capsule as a QR code or a file. Whoever receives it approves it again.

Show on another device

Send a capsule to a browser page or an ESP32 board on your desk. Pair once with a three-word code.

One menu for everything

Share, QR, Publish, Add to home screen and Send to device, one tap from any capsule.

A marketplace of tiny apps.

  • Browse and install capsules in the app or on the web.
  • Publish your own from any capsule's menu.
  • One-off novelties are hidden from the list, but still build when you ask.

Never code

A capsule is JSON. Its logic runs in our own interpreter: no loops, no network.

You decide

Allow or deny each permission. Say no, or press Back, and nothing is sent. Every block is logged, and one tap removes a capsule.

On the phone first

Most requests are built on the phone. When one needs a cloud model, the app names the provider first, which may be outside the EU.

Honest when it can't

Ask for live news and it tells you it can't fetch that yet, then suggests something it can build.

Built natively for HarmonyOS.

ArkTS and ArkUI

A native HarmonyOS app, written in ArkTS with ArkUI.

Home-screen widgets

Form Kit widgets with live state and buttons that work.

Calendar Kit

QR sharing

Scan Kit reads a shared capsule, and the receiver approves it again.

On-device AI

The Cactus engine, ported via Node-API, runs LFM2-VL-450M offline.

Dictation
